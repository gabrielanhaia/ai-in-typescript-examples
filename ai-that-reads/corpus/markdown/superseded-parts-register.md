# Superseded parts register

Parts that are no longer stocked, and what replaces them. Counter and support
staff should quote the successor, not the original, and should say plainly that
the original is discontinued rather than letting a customer wait for stock that
is not coming.

Fit is not guaranteed to be identical. Where the successor needs a different
adapter, spacer or bolt, it says so.

## How to read this table

The "superseded by" column is the part we would sell instead, not a
manufacturer's declared equivalent. Where the column says "none", there is no
successor in the Braxby range and the customer should be told so directly.

The "note" column is the part that matters at the counter. A successor that
needs a different bolt, a different olive or a different seatpost is a
successor that will generate a support ticket unless somebody says so at the
point of sale.

## What is not in this table

Parts that are simply out of stock are not listed here. Out of stock is a
stock question and the answer is on the product page.

Parts that were never stocked are not listed here either. If a customer asks
about a part number that does not appear anywhere in the catalogue and does not
appear in this register, the answer is that we have never sold it, and the
answer should say that rather than implying it was discontinued.
