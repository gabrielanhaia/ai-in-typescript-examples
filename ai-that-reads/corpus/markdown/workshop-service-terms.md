# Workshop service terms

These terms apply to any work booked into the Manchester workshop, whether the
bike was bought from Braxby Cycles or not. Prices are on the workshop price
list, which is displayed at the counter and issued with every quotation.

## Booking

Book online at `www.braxbycycles.example/workshop` or at the counter. We hold
slots for two working days after a booking is made and release them if the bike
has not arrived.

Bikes are booked in by the half day. A bike dropped off without a booking goes
into the queue behind everything already booked, and in season that can be two
weeks.

## Customer-supplied parts

This is the section customers argue with most, so it is stated as two rules
with no exceptions between them.

**The workshop will fit customer-supplied tyres, inner tubes, saddles,
seatposts, bar tape, pedals and bottle cages.** These are parts where a fitting
error is visible immediately, where the part cannot fail in a way that hurts
somebody a week later, and where we can inspect the part on the bench and see
what we are dealing with.

**The workshop will not fit customer-supplied disc brake pads, hydraulic hoses,
calipers, levers, rotors or any other braking component.** We have no way to
verify the compound, the backing plate, the hose fitting standard or the
provenance, and a brake that fails is not a brake that fails visibly. This is
not negotiable and the counter staff are told to decline it politely and
without discussion.

Customer-supplied drivetrain parts, wheels and frames are at the workshop
manager's discretion, decided on the bench, and declined without argument if
the part looks wrong for the bike.

Where the customer supplied the part, the labour guarantee in the warranty
policy does not apply. We will still put right our own mistake, but we will not
carry the risk of a part we did not sell.

## Quotations and authorisation

Every job gets a written quotation before work starts. If the work turns out to
need more than the quotation, we stop and call. We will not exceed a quotation
by more than £20 without speaking to the customer first, and we will not
proceed at all on a job where the extra work changes what the bike is for.

A customer who cannot be reached gets the bike back in the state it was in,
with the diagnostic time charged at the standard labour rate and nothing else.

## Collection and storage

Bikes are stored free for fourteen days after the completion call. After
fourteen days we charge storage per day. After ninety days, and after three
recorded attempts to contact the customer, we treat the bike as abandoned and
dispose of it under the Torts (Interference with Goods) Act. We have done this
twice in eleven years and would rather never do it again.

## Work we do not do

- We do not repair carbon frames or carbon components. We inspect them, and we
  refer them to a specialist repairer.
- We do not service internal-gear hubs beyond removing and refitting the wheel.
- We do not service electronic drivetrains beyond firmware updates, battery
  replacement and cable routing.
- We do not build wheels with customer-supplied second-hand spokes.
- We do not paint, respray or refinish.

## Bikes we will refuse

The workshop will decline a bike that is unsafe to work on: a cracked frame, a
seized steerer, a frame that has been repaired by an unknown party, or a bike
that arrives in a condition where the mechanic cannot get to the fasteners
without unreasonable cleaning. We will say so at the counter, we will explain
why, and we will not charge for looking.

## Warranty on workshop labour

Twelve months, as set out in the warranty policy. Bring it back and we redo it.
The exception for customer-supplied parts, above, is the only exception.
