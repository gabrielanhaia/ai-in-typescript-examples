# Staff handbook — 1. Opening and closing

Last updated: 6 January 2026
Applies to: Manchester workshop and trade counter

## Hours

The workshop and the trade counter keep the same hours.

- Tuesday to Friday: 09:00 to 17:30
- Saturday: 09:00 to 13:00
- Sunday and Monday: closed

Collections are taken during those hours only. There is no out-of-hours
collection and there is no key safe.

The workshop closes on 14 August every year for stock-taking. Online orders are
unaffected — they ship from the goods-in unit, which does not close.

## Opening

The first mechanic in does the following, in this order, and initials the sheet
on the back of the door.

1. Alarm off. If the alarm has triggered overnight, do not enter. Call the
   workshop manager from outside.
2. Lights and extraction on.
3. Check the overnight bike rack against the job board. Every bike on the rack
   should have a job card. A bike without a card goes on the manager's bench,
   not back on the rack.
4. Compressor on, drain the tank, check the line pressure.
5. Fill the parts washer if it is below the line.
6. Unlock the trade counter shutter at 09:00 and not before. Customers waiting
   outside at 08:50 are still waiting outside at 08:59.

## During the day

The counter is never left unattended while the shutter is up. If there is one
person on and they need to leave the counter, the shutter comes down and the
sign goes up.

Bikes brought in are booked into the job system before they touch the rack. A
bike on the rack without a record is a bike we will eventually lose.

## Closing

1. Every job either closed in the system or handed over on the board with a
   written note. "Someone on the late shift knows about it" is not a handover.
2. Tools back on the boards. The boards are shadowed; an empty shadow at
   closing time is a tool somebody has taken home by accident and it gets
   chased that evening, not the next week.
3. Compressor off and tank drained.
4. Waste oil and brake fluid into the labelled drums. Never into the sink, and
   never into general waste. This is section 2's subject and it is the one
   thing in this handbook that carries an outside legal consequence.
5. Bikes for collection moved to the secure rack and locked.
6. Shutter down, extraction off, lights off, alarm set.
7. Initial the sheet.

## Keys and alarm codes

Three sets of keys exist. The workshop manager holds one, the senior mechanic
holds one, and one is in the safe at the goods-in unit. Keys are not copied and
are not taken home by anyone else.

Alarm codes are individual. Sharing a code is a disciplinary matter. If you
have forgotten yours, the manager reissues it; do not borrow somebody else's
for the morning.

## When something is wrong at opening

A broken window, a forced shutter, a bike missing from the rack, or an alarm
that has triggered: do not enter, do not tidy, do not check whether anything is
missing. Call the workshop manager. The insurer and the police both want the
scene as it was found, and a well-meaning tidy-up has cost us a claim before.
