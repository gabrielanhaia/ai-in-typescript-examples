# Shipping and delivery

## Where we ship

Braxby Cycles ships to the United Kingdom, the Republic of Ireland and the
European Union. We do not ship anywhere else, and the checkout will not accept
an address outside those territories.

## United Kingdom

Standard delivery is priced by the weight of the whole basket, in three bands.

| Basket weight | Standard delivery |
|---|---|
| Up to 2 kg | £3.99 |
| Over 2 kg and up to 10 kg | £6.99 |
| Over 10 kg | £11.99 |

**Standard UK delivery is free on orders over £60.** The threshold is the order
value after any discount and before delivery, so a £62 basket with a 10%
discount code applied does not qualify.

Standard delivery is two to three working days. It is not a tracked service
below 2 kg; above 2 kg it is.

Next-day delivery is £7.99 regardless of weight, for orders placed before 14:00
Monday to Friday. An order placed at 14:01 on a Friday is picked on Monday and
delivered on Tuesday. We say this plainly because it is the single most common
complaint the support inbox receives.

We do not deliver on Sundays. Saturday delivery is available on the next-day
service for orders placed before 14:00 on a Friday, at £11.99.

## Republic of Ireland

A flat £9.99, three to five working days, tracked. Duties and taxes are paid by
us; the customer pays nothing on delivery.

## European Union

A flat £14.99, four to seven working days, tracked, delivered duty paid. Some
destinations are slower than the stated band and we do not guarantee the upper
figure for the islands.

## Click and collect

Orders can be collected from the Manchester workshop. Collection is free, there
is no minimum order, and the order is held for fourteen days. Collection is
available during workshop hours only, which are in the staff handbook and on
the workshop booking page — the trade counter and the workshop keep the same
hours.

The workshop closes on 14 August each year for stock-taking. Online orders are
unaffected and ship as normal from the Marbury Trading Estate unit; only
collections and workshop bookings are affected.

## Large and awkward items

Complete bikes, framesets and boxed wheelsets ship on a two-person service
where the carrier requires it. This can add a working day and it is quoted at
the £11.99 band rather than separately.

We cannot ship pressurised items — CO2 cartridges, sealed shocks — by air, so
those lines are UK and Republic of Ireland only.

## Missing and delayed parcels

Tracked services: raise it with us once the tracking has not moved for two
working days. Untracked services: we ask customers to wait ten working days
from dispatch before we treat a parcel as lost, because that is the point at
which the carrier will accept a claim.

A parcel marked as delivered but not received is a carrier investigation and
takes up to seven working days. We do not send a replacement before the
investigation closes, except on orders under £30, where we replace immediately
and absorb it.

## Delivery to a different address

We ship to any address in the permitted territories. Orders over £400 going to
an address other than the card's billing address are held for a verification
call. That call is made within one working day, and the order does not lose its
place in the pick queue while we make it.
