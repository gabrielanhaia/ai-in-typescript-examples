# Glossary of shop terms

Words that mean something specific inside Braxby Cycles. Where a term is also
defined in a policy document, that document wins and this entry is a summary.

**Backorder.** An order line we have accepted and cannot ship yet because the
stock has not arrived. Distinct from a special order, which we never held.

**Bare frame.** A frame or frameset sold on its own, with no drivetrain, wheels
or contact points. Matters for crash replacement, which covers bare frames and
does not cover frames sold inside a complete bike.

**Bed in.** The first few stops on a new pad and rotor pair, which transfer a
layer of pad material onto the rotor. A rotor that has been bedded in is a
consumable and cannot be returned.

**Build slot.** A half day of workshop time reserved for one job. The workshop
books by the slot, not by the hour.

**Consumable.** A part designed to wear out. The closed list is in the warranty
policy, section 4.

**Counter.** The trade counter attached to the Manchester workshop. Not a shop;
there is nothing to browse.

**Dispatch note.** The paper document in the box. It is not a VAT invoice; the
invoice is emailed.

**Elongation.** Chain wear, measured as a percentage. We replace 11- and
12-speed chains at 0.5%.

**Facing.** Machining a frame surface flat and square before a part is fitted —
a bottom bracket shell, a head tube, a disc mount. A faced frame is part of
what makes a build a workshop-built assembly.

**Goods in.** The receiving side of the Marbury Trading Estate unit, and by
extension the team who work there.

**Job sheet.** The record of a workshop job: what came in, what was quoted,
what was done, what was found. It is the document a warranty decision is made
from three years later, which is why it is written for a stranger.

**Non-stock item.** A line we order in against a customer order and do not hold
on the shelf. Trade returns of non-stock items carry a restocking charge.

**Olive.** The compression ring on a hydraulic hose fitting. Single use.
Changing a hose without a new olive and insert is the most common cause of a
leak that appears a week later.

**Pick queue.** The ordered list of paid orders waiting to be picked. Position
in the queue is by payment time, not by order time.

**Price promise.** Our retail price match. The conditions are in the price
promise document and all of them have to hold.

**Resaleable.** The condition a returned item has to be in: unfitted, complete,
in its original packaging, with nothing missing that we would have to source to
sell it again.

**Special order.** An item we order in specifically for one customer and would
not otherwise stock. Marked on the product page, and not returnable.

**Superseded.** A part we no longer stock, with a named replacement. The
register lists both.

**Torque figure.** The manufacturer's stated tightening figure for a fastener,
in newton metres. Workshop practice is that a figure exists for every fastener
we touch and that guessing is a disciplinary matter, not a style choice.

**Trade account.** A credit account for a business customer, on 30-day terms,
governed by the trade account terms rather than by the retail policies.

**True and tension.** Straightening a wheel and setting even spoke tension. A
workshop-built wheel comes back at 300 km for a first re-tension.

**Workshop-built assembly.** Anything the workshop assembled, laced, tensioned,
bled, faced or aligned before it left us. It carries its own warranty term,
which is not the component term.
