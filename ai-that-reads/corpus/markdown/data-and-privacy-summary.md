# Data and privacy — internal summary

A short internal summary of what we hold, why, and for how long. It is not the
published privacy notice and it is not legal advice. Where the two differ, the
published notice is the one that binds us and this document is the one that is
wrong.

## What we hold

**Order data.** Name, delivery address, billing address, email address, order
contents, order value, delivery method, carrier tracking reference. Held for
seven years from the order date because tax records require it.

**Account data.** Email address, hashed password, saved addresses, marketing
preferences, order history. Held until the customer closes the account, then
thirty days, then deleted except for the order data above.

**Payment data.** We do not hold card numbers. The payment processor holds
them; we hold a token, the last four digits and the card scheme, so that
support can identify which card an order was placed on.

**Workshop data.** Customer name, contact number, bike description, frame
number where the customer gives it, job sheet notes and photographs. Held for
three years from the job date, because a warranty question about a build can
arrive that late.

**Support data.** Email correspondence and ticket notes. Held for three years.

**Website analytics.** Aggregated page and session data with no name attached.
Held for fourteen months.

## What we do not hold

We do not hold health data, and staff must not record it on a job sheet even
when a customer volunteers it as context for a fitting question. We do not hold
date of birth. We do not hold anything about a customer's employer.

## Marketing

Marketing email goes only to customers who have opted in, and every message
carries an unsubscribe link that works on the first click without a login. An
unsubscribe is actioned within one working day and is not undone by a later
purchase.

We do not sell customer data, we do not share it with other retailers, and the
only third parties who receive it are the payment processor, the carriers, and
the email platform — each under a written processing agreement.

## Requests from customers

A customer can ask for a copy of what we hold, ask for it to be corrected, or
ask for it to be deleted. Requests go to `support@braxbycycles.example` and are
acknowledged within one working day and completed within one calendar month.

A deletion request does not delete the order data we are required to keep for
tax purposes. Say so plainly when acknowledging the request rather than
deleting the account and letting the customer discover the exception later.

## Staff obligations

- Do not export customer data to a personal device or a personal account.
- Do not discuss an order with anyone other than the person who placed it,
  including a spouse, without the account holder on the call.
- Do not photograph a job sheet.
- Report a suspected data incident to Customer Operations the same day, before
  attempting to fix it.

## Incidents

An incident is any unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure of customer data,
including sending an order confirmation to the wrong address. Customer
Operations logs it, assesses whether it is reportable, and reports it where the
threshold is met. The staff member who reports an incident is never the person
who is blamed for it, and that is stated here deliberately.
