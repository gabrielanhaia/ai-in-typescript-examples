# Price promise and discounts

## The price promise

If a customer finds the same item, new, in stock, from a UK retailer, at a
lower total price including delivery, we will match it. The match is applied at
the point of sale or refunded as the difference within seven days of purchase.

Conditions, all of which have to hold:

- Same manufacturer part number, not an equivalent.
- New and boxed, not ex-display, not returned stock, not a warranty
  replacement.
- In stock and available to order on the day, not on pre-order or backorder.
- A UK retailer with a UK-facing website and UK consumer terms.
- Total price including standard delivery to a UK mainland address.

We do not match auction sites, marketplace sellers trading through a larger
site, membership-only pricing, cashback offers, or a price that includes a
voucher code the customer has not yet earned.

We do not match our own past prices. A part that was cheaper last month is not
a price-match claim.

## Discount codes

One code per order. Codes do not stack, and the checkout will keep the larger
of two rather than applying both.

Codes apply to the basket value before delivery. A code that takes an order
below the £60 free-delivery threshold means delivery is charged; this is
correct behaviour and it is the second most common support question after
next-day cut-offs.

Codes do not apply to:

- Gift vouchers.
- Special-order items.
- Workshop labour.
- Anything already reduced in a sale, unless the code says otherwise in its own
  terms.

## Sale pricing

Sale prices are set by Buying and are live for the period stated on the
campaign. A customer who bought at full price within seven days of a sale
starting can have the difference refunded on request. Beyond seven days, no.

## Trade pricing

Trade account holders get trade prices, which are not visible to retail
customers and are not published. Trade pricing and the price promise do not
combine: a trade account holder is buying at trade and cannot also claim a
retail price match.

## Workshop pricing

Workshop labour is charged from the workshop price list. Labour is never
discounted, never price-matched, and never included in a promotional code. If a
customer asks why, the honest answer is that the labour rate is a wage cost and
there is no margin in it to give away.

## What staff may and may not offer

Support staff may apply a published code a customer forgot, refund a delivery
charge on an order that went wrong, and refund the difference on a valid price
promise. Anything else — a goodwill discount, a free upgrade, a waived
restocking charge — goes to a team lead. This is a small list on purpose; it
means a customer gets the same answer from whoever picks up the ticket.
