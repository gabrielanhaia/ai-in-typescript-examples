Book the workshop

Last reviewed: 20 January 2026

When we are open

Tuesday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:30. Saturday, 09:00 to 13:00. Closed Sunday and Monday. The trade counter keeps the same hours as the workshop.

We close on 14 August every year for stock-taking. No bookings are taken for that day and any job already booked is moved to the next working day.

How the booking works

Bikes are booked in by the half day. Choose a date, drop the bike off at the start of the slot, and we will call you when it is done or when we find something that changes the quotation.

We hold a slot for two working days after you book it. If the bike has not arrived by then the slot goes back into the pool, because an empty stand on a Saturday in June is a job somebody else wanted.

From April to September we are booking about two weeks ahead. In the winter it is usually a few days.

What to bring

  • The bike, obviously, and any parts you want fitted.
  • The make, model and year, if you know them. The year is the useful one — it is what tells us which standard we are dealing with.
  • Any battery, if it is an electric bike. Batteries come off before work starts and are stored in a steel cabinet.

Parts you bought elsewhere

We will fit customer-supplied tyres, inner tubes, saddles, seatposts, bar tape, pedals and bottle cages.

We will not fit customer-supplied braking components of any kind — pads, hoses, calipers, levers or rotors. We cannot verify what they are, and a brake that fails does not fail visibly.

Drivetrain parts, wheels and frames are the mechanic's call on the day. Where you supplied the part, our twelve-month labour guarantee does not apply.

Quotations

Every job is quoted in writing before we start. If the job turns out to need more than the quotation we stop and call, and we will not exceed a quotation by more than £20 without speaking to you.

Labour is charged from the workshop price list, which is on the wall at the counter and issued with every quotation. Labour is never discounted and is not included in any promotional code.

Collecting

Bikes are stored free for fourteen days after we call to say the job is done. After that we charge storage per day.
