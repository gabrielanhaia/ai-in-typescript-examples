Frequently asked questions

Last reviewed: 2 March 2026

Delivery

How much is delivery?

Standard UK delivery is £3.99 up to 2 kg, £6.99 from 2 kg to 10 kg, and £11.99 over 10 kg. It is free on orders over £60.

When is the cut-off for next-day delivery?

14:00, Monday to Friday. Next-day is £7.99 whatever the parcel weighs. Order at 14:01 on a Friday and it is picked on Monday for Tuesday.

Do you deliver on a Sunday?

No. Saturday delivery is available on the next-day service if you order before 14:00 on the Friday, and it is £11.99.

Where do you deliver to?

The UK, the Republic of Ireland and the EU. The checkout will not take an address outside those.

Can I collect?

Yes, from the Manchester workshop, free, with no minimum order. We hold a collection for fourteen days. Collection is during workshop hours only.

Returns

How long do I have to change my mind?

You have 30 days from the day your order arrives to send anything back, as long as it has not been fitted and you still have the packaging it came in.

What counts as fitted?

Bolted on, even for a minute. Cassettes mark the freehub splines, tyres show bead marks, and a frame with a bottom bracket pressed into it has been built. If you are not sure whether fitting something will cost you the return, ask us first.

Is the return postage free?

In the UK, yes — print the label from your account. From Ireland and the EU you pay the return postage unless the item is faulty.

How long does a refund take?

Five working days from the parcel reaching our returns unit, then however long your bank takes, which is usually another three.

Can I exchange something instead?

We do not do exchanges. Send it back for a refund and order the replacement now — that way the new one ships today instead of waiting for the old one to arrive.

Warranty

How long is the warranty?

Two years on components, five years on Wickhaven frames and rigid forks, and three years on anything our workshop built. Consumables are not covered.

What counts as a consumable?

Pads, tyres, tubes, sealant, chains, cassettes, chainrings, cables, bar tape, grips and bearings. Things designed to wear out.

I crashed my frame. Can I get a replacement?

If you bought the frame on its own from us, and it is less than three years old, we will sell you a replacement at 40% off. If the frame came as part of a complete bike, the scheme does not apply.

Workshop

Will you fit parts I bought somewhere else?

Tyres, tubes, saddles, seatposts, bar tape, pedals and bottle cages, yes. Brake parts of any kind, no — not pads, not hoses, not calipers. Drivetrain parts and wheels are up to the mechanic on the day.

How far ahead do I need to book?

Two weeks from April to September. A few days in the winter.

Do you work on carbon frames?

We service them. We do not repair them — a cracked carbon frame goes to a specialist repairer and we will tell you who.

Ordering

Can I use two discount codes?

No. One code per order, and the checkout keeps the better of the two if you try.

Do you price match?

Against a UK retailer, on the same part number, new and in stock, on the total price including delivery. Not marketplaces, not auction sites, not our own old prices.

My part number is not on the site any more.

It has probably been superseded. Ask us and we will tell you what replaced it and whether the fit is identical.
