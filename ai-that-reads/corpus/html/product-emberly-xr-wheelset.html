About this wheelset

The XR is the everyday alloy disc wheelset in the Emberly range: a 24 mm deep welded rim, straight-pull bladed spokes, and a hub that we have been able to get bearings for every year since we started selling it. It is not the lightest wheel in the range and it was not designed to be. It is the one that goes out on winter bikes, on commuters, and on any bike that spends time on a turbo.

Rims are tubeless ready and supplied taped, with valves in the box. The rim bed is hooked, so tyre choice is not restricted and the pressure ceiling is the tyre's rather than the rim's.

Hub and freehub

Centre lock rotor fitting. The freehub body is replaceable and both the 11-speed and 12-speed bodies are stocked as spares. Bearings are sealed cartridge and are replaced rather than serviced, which is the workshop's standard practice for this hub.

Front hubs from the 2024 batch (part number WHL-5104) were subject to a recall in November 2025. WHL-5120 is the current part and is not affected. If you own a wheelset bought before December 2025, check the recall notice.
