About this tyre

The Gravel 45 replaced the Gravel 40 in the Draycott range and it is a wider tyre in every sense: a taller casing, a squarer profile, and a tread that runs a file pattern down the centre with shoulder blocks that only do anything once the bike is leaned over. On tarmac it is quiet. On dry hardpack it is quick. In deep mud it clogs, and it is not pretending otherwise.

It is a tubeless tyre and we would not recommend running it with a tube. The casing is supple enough that a tube undoes most of what you are paying for, and at these pressures a pinch flat is the most likely thing to end a ride.

Clearance

45 mm is genuinely 45 mm on a 21 mm internal rim, and it grows a little after a few rides. If your frame is rated for 45 mm exactly, measure before ordering. The Gravel 40 it replaced is not coming back, so a frame that will not take 45 needs a different tyre rather than the previous size.

Tyres are consumables. They are covered against a defect present on arrival and not against wear. A tyre that has been on a rim has been fitted and cannot be returned, so check the clearance first.

Hookless rims: this tyre is not on the approved list for every hookless rim on the market. If your rim is hookless, check the rim manufacturer's list before ordering, and do not exceed the rim's maximum pressure even though the tyre is rated to 4.0 bar.
