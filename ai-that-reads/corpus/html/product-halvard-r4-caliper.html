About this caliper

The R4 is the four-piston caliper in the Halvard range. It is the one to fit if you are heavy on the brakes, if you ride loaded, or if you spend your descents dragging a lever. Against the two-piston R2 it gives more pad area and a longer service life at the cost of weight and a slightly firmer initial bite.

The body is a two-piece forging with a bridge across the pistons, which is what keeps the flex out of it. Pistons are phenolic, which transfers less heat into the fluid than an alloy piston does. The caliper takes the standard Halvard pad shape and is supplied with one set of sintered pads.

Fitting

Post mount as supplied. Fitting to a 160 mm rotor on a 160 mm mount needs no adapter. Fitting a larger rotor, or fitting to a flat-mount frame, needs the correct adapter for the position — the adapters are listed under brake adapters and the fit is not interchangeable between front and rear.

Hose fitting is the current Halvard standard and is not compatible with the 2019 R2 body without a new olive and insert.
