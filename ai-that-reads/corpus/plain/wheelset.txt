The XR is the everyday alloy disc wheelset in the Emberly range: a 24 mm deep welded rim, straight-pull bladed spokes, and a hub that we have been able to get bearings for every year since we started selling it. It is not the lightest wheel in the range and it was not designed to be. It is the one that goes out on winter bikes, on commuters, and on any bike that spends time on a turbo.
