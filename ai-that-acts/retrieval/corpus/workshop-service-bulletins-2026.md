# Workshop service bulletins, 2026

Bulletins are issued by the workshop manager and are binding on workshop staff
from the date on the bulletin. They are cumulative: a bulletin stays in force
until a later one supersedes it or the year closes.

Read the current year's bulletins before starting a job you have not done in a
while.

---

## 2026-01 — Torque wrench calibration

All torque wrenches on the bench are now sent for calibration every six months
rather than annually. Two wrenches were found out of tolerance during the
December audit, both reading low, which is the direction that produces
under-torqued stem bolts and a customer who comes back.

Calibration stickers carry the due date. A wrench past its date does not get
used, and there is no judgement call available here.

---

## 2026-02 — Tubeless setup on hookless rims

Hookless rims have a hard pressure ceiling and a narrow list of approved tyres.
Do not set up a tubeless tyre on a hookless rim unless the tyre is on the rim
manufacturer's approved list, and do not exceed the rim's stated maximum
pressure even if the tyre is rated higher.

If the customer insists on a tyre that is not on the list, decline the job and
explain why. Record the refusal on the job sheet.

---

## 2026-03 — Bleed procedure, wet-weather bikes

The service intervals document now calls for a six-month bleed on bikes used
for wet-weather commuting. Workshop staff should ask the question at booking —
"is this a year-round bike?" — and set the next-service reminder accordingly
rather than defaulting to twelve months.

The reminder is set in the job system at the point the job is closed, not at
the point it is booked.

---

## 2026-04 — Rear caliper mount adapters, Halvard R4

A recurring error on the bench: fitting a 160 mm rear rotor to a frame with a
post mount designed for 180 mm, and then reaching for a generic adapter to make
up the difference.

Use the correct adapter. For a Halvard R4 rear caliper going from a 160 mm
mount to a 180 mm rotor, the part is **BRK-1180**. It is the only rear adapter
we stock that has the correct offset for the R4 caliper body, and it ships with
the two longer bolts the conversion needs. A generic post-mount adapter will
bolt up and will hold the caliper a millimetre out of plane, which is why those
bikes come back with a rub the customer can hear and the mechanic cannot find.

Front conversions use ADP-3308 and are unaffected.

If the part is out of stock, the job waits. Do not substitute.

---

## 2026-05 — Chain wear gauges

The 0.5% gauges have replaced the 0.75% gauges on all three benches. Eleven-
and twelve-speed chains are replaced at 0.5% elongation, and a chain that
passes the old gauge has usually already started on the cassette.

Old gauges have been removed from the workshop. If you find one in a toolbox,
bin it.

---

## 2026-06 — Customer-supplied braking components

A reminder rather than a change. The workshop service terms are clear that we
do not fit customer-supplied braking components of any kind, and that includes
pads that a customer has bought from us previously and brought back on a
different bike.

Three jobs were accepted against this rule in the first quarter. All three were
completed and none of them caused a problem, which is exactly why the rule
needs restating: nothing going wrong is not evidence that the rule was wrong.

---

## 2026-07 — Stock-taking closure

The workshop closes on 14 August for stock-taking, as it does every year. No
bookings are taken for that day. Bikes already in the workshop are secured and
not worked on. Online orders are unaffected and ship as normal from the goods-
in unit.

Any job scheduled for the closure day is moved to the following working day,
and the customer is called rather than emailed.
