# Returns and refunds

This is the customer-facing returns policy. The internal procedure the returns
desk follows is in the staff handbook and it carries detail this document does
not.

## The window

**Customers may return an unused item within 30 days of delivery for a full
refund.** The item must be unfitted, in resaleable condition, and in its
original packaging with any tags, backing card or instruction sheet still with
it.

Thirty days is a policy, not a statutory minimum, and it is deliberately longer
than the statutory cooling-off period so that nobody has to count weekends.

## What "unfitted" means

A part is unfitted if it has not been mounted on a bicycle. In practice:

- A caliper that has been bolted on, even briefly, is fitted.
- A cassette that has been on a freehub body has almost always marked the
  splines and is fitted.
- A chain that has been sized or run through a drivetrain is fitted.
- Tyres are fitted the moment they have been on a rim. A tyre that has been
  test-fitted and removed will show bead marks and we will see them.
- A frame that has had a bottom bracket or headset pressed into it is fitted.
- Clothing and helmets may be tried on. A helmet with a scuffed shell or a
  compressed liner is not resaleable.

If you are not sure whether fitting a part will cost you the return, ask before
you fit it. The answer is usually yes.

## Items we cannot take back

- Anything cut to length: cables, outers, hydraulic hose, steerer tubes, bar
  tape that has been unwound and applied.
- Tubeless sealant and any opened liquid.
- Special-order items, which are marked as such on the product page before the
  order is placed.
- Gift vouchers.
- Anything returned without its original packaging where that packaging is part
  of how we resell it — a boxed wheelset, for example.

## How to return something

1. Start a return at `www.braxbycycles.example/returns` with the order number
   and the email address on the order.
2. Print the prepaid label. UK returns are free. Republic of Ireland and EU
   returns are at the customer's cost unless the item is faulty.
3. Pack the item as it arrived and attach the label to the outside of the
   outer box, not to the product box.
4. Drop it at any carrier point.

## Refunds

A refund is issued to the original payment method within five working days of
the return arriving at the returns unit. Card refunds usually clear within a
further three working days; that part is the bank's timetable, not ours.

We refund the original outbound standard delivery charge when the whole order
is returned. We do not refund the difference where the customer chose next-day
delivery, and we do not refund the outbound charge on a partial return.

## Exchanges

We do not process exchanges as a single transaction. Return the item for a
refund and place a new order. This is slower to describe and faster in
practice, because the new order ships as soon as it is placed rather than
waiting for the return to arrive.

## Faulty items

A faulty item is not a return, it is a warranty claim, and the warranty policy
governs it. The practical difference for the customer is that carriage is
always ours and the 30-day window does not apply.

If an item arrives damaged in transit, tell us within three working days of
delivery so that we can raise it with the carrier. After that the carrier will
not accept the claim and we have to treat it as a warranty question instead,
which is slower for everyone.

## Trade accounts

Trade returns are governed by the trade account terms and not by this document.
The headline difference is that trade returns of non-stock items carry a
restocking charge.
